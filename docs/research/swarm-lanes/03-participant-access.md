# Swarm Lane 3 — DTC/NSCC Participant Access

**Agent:** [Participant access](c0d80891-b00e-4505-bbb8-beb2e9d3f9d2)

### Who can participate

**DTC** — Organizations approved under **Rule 3** (broker-dealers, banks/trust companies, clearance firms, insurers, registered investment companies, pension funds, or others showing material benefit). Participants Fund + Settling Bank typical. Classes: Participants, Limited Participants, Pledgees.

**NSCC** — Members: registered B/D; bank/trust company; registered clearing agency; insurer; registered investment company; or Board-approved material benefit. Members / Limited / Sponsored.

**Not eligible:** natural persons / retail individuals as DTC/NSCC members.

### Accounts (high level)

- Direct participants hold securities + settlement accounts on DTC; money via Settling Bank.
- Beneficial owners below participants (DRS or street name). **Cede & Co.** = record owner of fungible bulk.
- Indirect participants access only via a direct participant.
- NSCC clears broker-to-broker; CNS needs DTC eligibility.

### Retail vs participant

| | Retail individual | DTC/NSCC participant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Membership | Cannot apply | Contractual member |
| DTC systems | No direct access | Settlement, custody, pledging |
| Holdings | DRS or street name via broker | Participant accounts / omnibus |
| NSCC | No | Clearing, CNS, Clearing Fund |

**Ceiling:** Public docs establish access tiers; they do **not** prove a personal “account at DTC in your name.”
