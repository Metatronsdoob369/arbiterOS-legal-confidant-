## 2025-02-18 - [Library Memoization]
**Learning:** Extracting complex inline UI components (like the `LibraryItem` in `Library.tsx`) into a separate component wrapped with `React.memo` is critical to prevent O(N) re-renders during high-frequency events, like typing in the search bar. However, it requires ensuring all passed callbacks (e.g., `togglePin`, `deleteItem`) are wrapped in `React.useCallback` and that their dependency chains (like `loadItems`) are also stable, otherwise the memoization breaks.
**Action:** Always verify the stability of the full callback dependency chain when adding `React.memo`, or use a custom comparison function for `React.memo` (`(prev, next) => prev.item.id === next.item.id`) for bulletproof protection against unstable references.
